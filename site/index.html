Agent Pioneer Badge

A free, non-transferable identity badge for Agent ID holders on BNB Chain. One badge per Agent ID. Claiming is free — you only pay the network gas. The badge has no monetary value and promises no returns.

Not deployed yet

The badge contract has not been deployed to BNB Chain yet. Check back after launch.

1. Connect your wallet

Use your injected wallet (Binance Wallet or MetaMask) on BNB Chain (chain ID 56). Nothing is sent to any server — this page talks only to your wallet and the public chain.

2. Check your eligibility

Connect your wallet to see whether it holds an Agent ID.

3. Claim your badge

Free — the claim costs 0 value. You only pay gas. Your wallet will ask you to confirm the transaction; nothing is sent until you approve it.
